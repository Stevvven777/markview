# Webb’s First Deep Field

***Webb's First Deep Field*** is the first [operational image](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operational_image "Operational image") taken by the [James Webb Space Telescope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Webb_Space_Telescope "James Webb Space Telescope") &#40;JWST&#41;\. The [deep-field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_deep_fields "List of deep fields") photograph, which covers a tiny area of sky visible from the [Southern Hemisphere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Hemisphere "Southern Hemisphere"), is centered on [SMACS 0723](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SMACS_0723 "SMACS 0723"), a [galaxy cluster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galaxy_cluster "Galaxy cluster") in the [constellation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constellation "Constellation") of [Volans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volans "Volans")\. Thousands of [galaxies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galaxies "Galaxies") are visible in the image, some as old as 13 billion years\.[&#91;1&#93;](#cite_note-1) It is the highest-resolution image of the [early universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Early_universe "Early universe") ever taken\. Captured by the telescope's [Near-Infrared Camera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NIRCam "NIRCam") &#40;NIRCam&#41;, the image combined different images taken with multiple filters\. The image was revealed to the public by [NASA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASA "NASA") on 11 July 2022\.

## Background

The [James Webb Space Telescope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Webb_Space_Telescope "James Webb Space Telescope") is a [space telescope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_telescope "Space telescope") operated by [NASA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASA "NASA") and designed primarily to conduct [infrared astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infrared_astronomy "Infrared astronomy")\. Launched in December 2021, the spacecraft has been in a [halo orbit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halo_orbit "Halo orbit") around the second Sun–Earth [Lagrange point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lagrange_point "Lagrange point") &#40;L2&#41;, about 1\.5 million kilometers &#40;900,000 mi&#41; from Earth, since January 2022\. At L2, the [gravitational pull](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gravity#Newton's_theory_of_gravitation "Gravity") of the Sun combines with the gravitational pull of the Earth to produce an orbital period that matches Earth's, and the Earth and Sun remain co-aligned &#40;as seen from that point&#41; as the Earth and the spacecraft orbit the Sun together\.[&#91;2&#93;](#cite_note-:2-2)

*Webb's First Deep Field* was taken by the telescope's [Near-Infrared Camera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NIRCam "NIRCam") &#40;NIRCam&#41; and is a composite produced from images at different [wavelengths](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wavelength "Wavelength"), totalling 12\.5 hours of [exposure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exposure_(photography) "Exposure &#40;photography&#41;") time\.[&#91;3&#93;](#cite_note-:0-3)[&#91;4&#93;](#cite_note-:1-4)

[SMACS 0723](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SMACS_J0723.3-7327 "SMACS J0723\.3-7327") is a galaxy cluster visible from Earth's [Southern Hemisphere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Hemisphere "Southern Hemisphere"),[&#91;5&#93;](#cite_note-5) and has often been examined by Hubble and other telescopes in search of the deep past\.[&#91;2&#93;](#cite_note-:2-2)
