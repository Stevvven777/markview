# A little room to read

Some documents deserve more than a quick glance. A field note, a chapter,
an explanation you want to understand: open the file, find your place, and
let the page hold your attention.

## Ideas, clearly expressed

Good typography gives **important ideas** a clear voice and *quiet details*
room to breathe. A formula belongs to the sentence around it: $e^{i\pi}+1=0$.
Longer arguments can take a line of their own.

$$
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2}\,dx = \sqrt{\pi}
$$

> Keep the page quiet. Let the ideas speak.

## From notes to working code

```rust
fn reading_time(words: usize) -> usize {
    words.div_ceil(240)
}
```

A table, a footnote, a linked chapter: each has its place. Keep reading at your
own pace, in the light and type that suit you.
